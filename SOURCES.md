# Sources for 25 November 2026

Short notes for the news image. Each item is an event on 25 November 2026, or a claim first carried that day. Disputed points stay attached to the outlet or official who made them.

## Strait of Hormuz

Iran's IRGC Navy said on Wednesday 25 November 2026 that two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded, and caught fire while trying to use what it called an unauthorized route off the southern and eastern shores of the Strait of Hormuz. Reuters reported the statement from Iranian state media. The IRGC named no ships, flags, owners, or crews, and it did not describe the scale of damage. Iran International reported that the claims could not be independently verified. UKMTO has separately described some recent Hormuz incidents as ships struck by unidentified projectiles, which does not match the mine wording. The mine strikes remain an official Iranian claim, not a confirmed fact.

- [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/iran-says-ships-hit-mines-in-hormuz-warns-vessels-to-coordinate-passage-3013942)
- [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611254354)

## Ras Tanura

On Wednesday morning, Saudi-led coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said air defences intercepted and destroyed four drones over Ras Tanura in the Eastern Province. He said fragments fell, an initial assessment found no casualties, and he blamed Yemen's Houthis. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree claimed the group attacked the Ras Tanura refinery and caused fires. That damage claim was not independently verified, and the Saudi statement did not confirm that the refinery itself was hit. A drone strike on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District the day before, which injured 11 people, is 24 November context only.

## Gaza City

Early on Wednesday 25 November 2026, an Israeli airstrike killed Mohammad — also written Muhammad Abu al-Ouf or Abuelouf — a Hamas internal-security official, in an apartment in the Shati (Beach) refugee camp west of Gaza City. Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry and medics said his wife and two children were also killed. The Israeli military later confirmed that it had killed the official.

## Ukraine

Overnight into Wednesday, Russian strikes damaged Ukrainian energy and gas infrastructure. DTEK said a thermal power plant stopped generating electricity after a heavy attack significantly damaged its equipment. DTEK did not name the plant or its location. A Naftogaz gas-production site in the Poltava region was reported to have caught fire and stopped operations. Ukrainian officials said the attacks caused electricity outages in five regions.

## Jiuquan

China launched Shenzhou-24 at 08:18 Beijing time on 25 November 2026 from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center on a Long March-2F rocket. The China Manned Space Agency called the launch a complete success. The crew is commander Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi, and Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut to visit the Tiangong station. Reuters reported that the crew entered Tiangong at 14:35 Beijing time (06:35 GMT), about six hours after lift-off. Ali is expected to stay about five to ten days and return on Shenzhou-23.

## Cartagena

Early on Wednesday 25 November 2026, Colombian security forces freed the remaining hostages, about 20 shoppers and employees, at a supermarket in Cartagena. That ended a siege of about 17 hours that began on Tuesday morning after what President Abelardo de la Espriella said was a failed attempt to rob an armoured car. The president said the gunmen had no political affiliation. One gunman was killed and four were arrested. About 10 hostages had already been released on Tuesday.

- [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckp80mjr9emyo)
- [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/25/hostages-freed-colombia-security-forces-storm-cartagena-supermarket)

Associated Press also reported the Cartagena siege the same day. The AP article slug names the supermarket chain, so that URL is omitted here.
